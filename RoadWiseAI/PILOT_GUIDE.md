# RoadWise AI: Supervised Field Pilot Protocol (Stage 7)

This protocol guides municipal road engineers, supervisors, and field inspection crews through conducting a controlled, supervised operational pilot of the **RoadWise AI** Web GIS platform across the study area of **Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India**.

---

## 1. Important Operating Disclaimers

### A. Decision Support Only
> **Statutory Notice:** All artificial intelligence (YOLOv8) defect classifications, bounding boxes, and multi-criteria priority rankings generated by RoadWise AI are assistive **decision-support tools** intended for human engineer review. They do not constitute official statutory civil engineering assessments, structural evaluations, or legally binding PWD certifications. Final maintenance sign-offs require on-site engineer verification.

### B. Sample vs Operational GIS Datasets
> **Data Provenance:** The sample road network (`road_segments`), junction points (`junctions`), and AADT traffic counts (`traffic_counts`) provided by `import_gis_data.py --sample` are synthetic demonstration baselines constructed for Thiruvananthapuram. For full municipal deployment, live operational GIS datasets must be provided by Kerala PWD, NATPAC, or local municipal bodies and imported via `import_gis_data.py --roads <path> --source "Kerala PWD 2026"`.

## 2. Pilot Participant Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Platform Access Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Field Observer / Citizen** | Captures road defects on mobile devices; records GPS and descriptions. | Public web access (submit report, view map). |
| **Road Inspection Engineer** | Reviews field photo and AI suggestions; confirms damage type and severity. | Public web access or Authenticated Staff. |
| **Maintenance Supervisor** | Reviews GIS priority ranking; generates work orders; assigns crews and targets. | **Authenticated Staff / Admin** (`@login_required`). |
| **Field Maintenance Crew** | Executes road repair (patching, resurfacing); reports progress and completion. | **Authenticated Staff** (updates work-order status). |
| **Chief Municipal Engineer** | Oversees maintenance queue, audits status timeline, exports official PDF work orders. | **Authenticated Admin** (full system access). |

## 3. End-to-End Supervised Pilot Workflow

The following step-by-step workflow models a complete maintenance cycle for an incident located along the **Pattom – Kowdiar arterial corridor** in Thiruvananthapuram:

```
  [1. Capture Photo & GPS]
             │
             ▼
  [2. AI Defect Detection] ──► Human Review & Confirmation
             │
             ▼
  [3. Submit Report to GIS]
             │
             ▼
  [4. Multi-Criteria Prioritisation] ──► Score: 0–100, Tier: High/Med/Low
             │
             ▼
  [5. Supervisor Generates Work Order] ──► WO-0001 (Open)
             │
             ▼
  [6. Crew Allocation & Scheduling] ──► Assigned ➔ In Progress
             │
             ▼
  [7. Progress Updates & After-Photos] ──► Upload Photos/PDFs & Publish (Stage 8)
             │
             ▼
  [8. Complete & Audit Verification] ──► Completed (Mandatory Summary)
             │
             ▼
  [9. Official Work-Order Export] ──► Printable PDF, CSV, GeoJSON (RFC 7946)
```

---

### Step 1: Capture Incident Photo & Location
1. The field observer takes a clear JPEG photograph of the road defect (e.g. pothole or crocodile cracking) using a GPS-enabled smartphone or camera along an inspection route in Thiruvananthapuram.
2. In the RoadWise AI web interface, upload the photograph in the **Report Road Damage** panel.
3. Click **Extract GPS from Photo**:
   - The platform extracts EXIF metadata (e.g. Latitude `8.524100° N`, Longitude `76.936600° E`).
   - The interactive Leaflet map automatically centers on the defect location with an inspection pin.

### Step 2: Review AI Suggestions & Human Confirmation
1. Click **Detect Damage with AI**.
2. The local YOLOv8 detector evaluates the image and highlights detected defect bounding boxes (e.g. `Pothole: 87% confidence`).
3. The platform suggests an initial defect class and severity.
4. **Human Review Step:** The field engineer inspects the road conditions and confirms or adjusts:
   - **Damage Type:** Select confirmed type (e.g. `Pothole`, `Alligator crack`, `Surface deterioration`).
   - **Severity:** Confirm appropriate engineering severity (`Critical`, `Moderate`, `Minor`).
   - **Road Name:** Enter physical street name (e.g. `Pattom - Kowdiar Road`).
   - **Notes:** Add context (e.g. `Severe edge drop-off near drainage inlet`).

### Step 3: Submit Report into PostgreSQL/PostGIS
1. Click **Submit Road-Damage Report**.
2. The platform stores the report in PostgreSQL, projects the coordinates into UTM Zone 43N (`EPSG:32643`), and saves the photo into `static/uploads/`.
3. The interactive map places a permanent marker, and the incident card appears in the live feed.

### Step 4: Multi-Criteria GIS Prioritisation
1. The platform automatically evaluates the incident against the spatial decision-support engine:
   - **Confirmed Severity:** Critical (+40 pts)
   - **Road Classification:** Matched State Highway (+20 pts)
   - **Traffic Volume:** High AADT corridor (+15 pts)
   - **Junction Proximity:** Within 200 m of intersection (+5 pts)
   - **Repeat Clustering:** Spatial proximity to other reports (+3 pts)
   - **Report Age:** Newly logged (+0 pts)
2. The report receives a combined **Priority Score (83 / 100)** and a **High Priority Tier** badge.
3. The card displays a transparent breakdown explaining every point contributed.

### Step 5: Generate Maintenance Work Order
1. In the top navigation, the maintenance supervisor clicks **🔐 Staff Login** and enters their credentials (`officer_pattom`).
2. In the report feed, check the box next to the newly submitted report.
3. Click **⚡ Create Work Order(s)**.
4. The system creates a linked work order with a stable code (e.g. `WO-0001`) in `Open` status and records the initial creation audit entry.

### Step 6: Dispatch & Lifecycle Tracking
1. Scroll to the **Maintenance Work-Order Queue** panel.
2. Locate `WO-0001` and click **Update** (or click **View Work Order** on the report feed card).
3. In the Work Order Management Modal:
   - Assign the task: enter `Zone 2 Asphalt Patch Crew`.
   - Set target completion date: `2026-11-02`.
   - Update lifecycle status from `Open` to `Assigned`.
   - Enter status note: `Dispatched cold mix patch team for immediate edge repair.`
4. Click **💾 Save Changes**.
5. When the crew arrives on site, the supervisor transitions status to `In Progress`.

### Step 7: Record Field Progress Updates & Attach Evidence (Stage 8)
1. While work is actively underway, the assigned officer or maintenance supervisor opens the work order modal.
2. In the **Post Field Progress Update** section:
   - **Work Completed / Milestone:** Enter specific repair actions taken (e.g. `Pavement edge cleared; tack coat applied; asphalt cold-patch laid`).
   - **Progress Percentage:** Update current completion percentage (e.g. `60%`).
   - **Blockers / Next Actions:** Record any delays (e.g. `Monsoon shower delay`) and planned follow-ups.
   - **Attachments:** Upload an in-progress field photo (JPEG/PNG) or engineer sign-off document (PDF) (max 10 MB).
   - **Internal Staff Notes:** Record private crew or vendor notes. *Note: These are strictly excluded from the public feed.*
   - **Publish to Public Feed:** Check this box so the update is immediately visible to citizens on the public progress board.
3. Click **🚀 Post Update**.
4. The timeline updates immediately, displaying the milestone, progress meter, and attachment thumbnail.
5. If an update requires a minor factual correction later, submit a correction update with the **Correction of earlier update** checkbox checked to preserve full append-only audit history.

### Step 8: Complete Maintenance & Mandatory Verification Summary
1. Once physical road patching is completed and inspected, open the work order modal.
2. Select status `Completed` (either in the work-order edit form or the progress update form).
3. **Mandatory Completion Summary:** Enter the final engineering completion description (e.g. `Pothole filled and compacted with 50mm hot asphalt mix. Verified level with road surface and reopened to traffic.`). Transition to `Completed` will be rejected if this summary is blank.
4. Optionally attach a final post-repair inspection photo.
5. Save changes / post update. The system automatically populates `completed_at` with the current UTC timestamp, transitions status, and publishes the final milestone to the public feed.

### Step 9: Public Transparency & Export Deliverables
1. **Public Road Repair Feed:** Citizens visiting the main dashboard can scroll to **Road Repair Progress** to view published milestones, road locations, and verified after-work photos without logging in.
2. **Interactive Map Linking:** Citizens or supervisors can click **📍 View on Map** on any public card to pan and zoom directly to the repaired road segment.
3. **Official Maintenance Exports:**
   - **Printable PDF Work Order (`/api/export/pdf`):** Formal engineering document with defect photo, priority score meter, factor breakdown, and disclaimer.
   - **Office Tracking CSV (`/api/export/csv`):** Spreadsheet equipped with `Work Order ID`, `Work Order Status`, and OWASP formula injection protection.
   - **GIS FeatureCollection GeoJSON (`/api/export/geojson`):** RFC 7946 compliant GeoJSON `[longitude, latitude]` for immediate integration into municipal QGIS or ArcGIS systems.

## 4. Key Decisions for Production Transition

Prior to transitioning from this controlled local pilot to an enterprise municipal deployment:
1. **Official PWD Road Network:** Replace synthetic sample data with GIS shapefiles from Kerala Public Works Department.
2. **Official Traffic Counts:** Integrate live NATPAC or municipal sensor feeds.
3. **Enterprise Identity Integration:** Connect authentication to municipal SSO (LDAP / Active Directory / OAuth2) if centralized login is mandated.
4. **HTTPS / TLS Deployment:** Terminate SSL via reverse proxy (e.g. NGINX or IIS) with an official TLS certificate before exposing beyond the local municipal intranet.
